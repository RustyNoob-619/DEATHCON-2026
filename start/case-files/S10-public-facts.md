# S10. What HTA publishes

*Case file · start set · TLP:CLEAR · supplied by the Council's liaison*

What HTA, Quillfeather Controls and the Floe Assembly say in public. Nothing else about HTA's plant is public.

## Hollowvent Thermal Authority

- Runs **Vent Station Tern**, which powers and heats Hollow Rookery from the vent turbines.
- Publishes its **quarterly maintenance calendar**. The window of the fourth quarter is **Thursday 8 October 2026**.
- Says that its research team works with controller vendors. It does not say how many turbines it runs, what the units are called, or what the team's projects are.

## Quillfeather Controls

- Makes the **QF-400 governor controller** and the engineering software **QF Studio**.
- Publishes firmware release notes on its support site, **`quillfeather.invalid`**. The latest published version is **4.2.0** (17 March 2026). Version 4.2.1 has not been announced.
- States that it never sends update procedures by email.

## Floe Assembly

- Publishes the daily **Tidewatch bulletin** (tides and ice conditions) at `tidewatch.invalid`.
- Publishes the Infrastructure Council's incident-reporting guidance.

## Not published

The unit-level names inside HTA, HTA's research projects and trial equipment, and the serial formats of its controllers.
