# Template: the assessment statement

In 200 words or fewer.

1. **Judgement.** Who is most likely behind this?
2. **Confidence.** One word: low, moderate or high.
3. **Key evidence.** The two or three items that carry the judgement, each with its layer and cost to fake.
4. **Alternatives.** The hypotheses you rejected and why.
5. **What would change my mind.**

Then, outside the 200 words: **five collection steps** (in the platform and beyond), and **what you would tell the Council to do tomorrow, and what you would not tell them.**
