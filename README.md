# Hollowvent: the analysts' pack

*Case HTA-2026-1005 · a SWORD Capstone · everything in this pack is invented.*

This is what you are handed at the start of the watch. Read the pages in this order:

1. `start/01-briefing.md`: the tasking, the preliminary incident summary, what you are working with and the Rules of Engagement.
2. `start/02-glossary.md`: the world's words in plain terms.
3. `start/03-tasks.md`: the tasks, in order, with their start times.
4. `guide/analyst-guide.md`: how a Capstone works, what the Pebble Lab can see and the rules that come with the pack. Keep it open.

The platform's Capstone section shows pages 1 to 3, so you can read them beside the tools. Open the case files in `start/case-files/` as the tasks call for them. More case files arrive during the watch: the facilitator will tell you when. The five adversary profiles are in `start/profiles/`, the Skuan word list is `start/04-skuan-words.md`, and the templates for your boards are in `start/templates/`.

**Before you begin:** this pack describes records in the platform's Hold, among real samples. Never execute anything from the Hold, and do not upload anything from it to a public service. Do not use open-source intelligence to complete this exercise.
